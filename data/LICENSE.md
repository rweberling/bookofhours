## What the licence covers

The metadata: the records' identifiers, citations and bibliography lines,
edition details and their identifiers (DOI, HathiTrust, OCLC, Open Library,
Wikidata and the like), people and their Wikidata links, dates, places,
tags and vocabularies, links, rights statements, captions and alt text,
and the selection and arrangement of the records, in:

`readings.json`, `images.json`, `species.json`, `phenomena.json`,
`vocab.json`, `postcards.csv`, `lectio-data.json` (the posts' records),
`image-files.json`, `site.json` and `retired-ids.json`.

## What it doesn't cover

These keep their own terms, as the repository's [LICENSE](../LICENSE) sets
out:

- **Ryan Weberling's own writing**: the text of each piece in
  `writing.json` (headnotes, supplementa, versicles, phenomenon lines and
  the rest), the Lectio Terra posts as written (`substack/`), and the
  site's other prose. © Ryan Weberling, all rights reserved.
- **The texts and images the collection quotes or reproduces**: each
  reading's quoted passage (its `text`) and the image files. Works in the
  public domain stay in the public domain; works still in copyright are
  quoted here by permission or as fair use, and are not relicensed by
  this licence. Each record's `rights` says what's known.
- The site's code, design, fonts and the Earthly Star glyph.
