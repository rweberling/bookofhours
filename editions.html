Horae Terrenae

Otherwise Editions

In 2027, Otherwise Editions will begin publishing The Earthly Book of Hours in print and in digital editions: a volume for the Hours, one for each season of the year, and a weather-themed collation.

Each volume will come with a key that opens your digital edition: a single, self-contained file that grows as volumes are added, opens in any web browser, works offline, and is meant to still open in years to come. It never reports back when you read it, and your key works on your own device — at least, that is the blueprint design.

To hear when editions are ready, write to editions@earthlyhours.com, subscribe to Lectio Terra on Substack, or join the Otherwise space on DFOS.
